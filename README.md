## 📌 Overview

**AI Revenue Detective** is an analytics and business intelligence application designed to investigate changes in business revenue and identify the major dimensions contributing to those changes.

Traditional dashboards are useful for answering **"What happened?"**, but understanding **"Why did it happen?"** often requires analysts to manually explore multiple datasets and business dimensions.

This project combines **Python, data analytics, root-cause analysis, Generative AI, and Streamlit** to create an interactive revenue investigation workflow.

Users can select a month, investigate its revenue performance, identify the major contributing categories, and generate an AI-assisted business explanation based on validated analytical evidence.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

When revenue changes, business teams need answers beyond a simple KPI.

Typical questions include:

- What changed?
- How significant was the change?
- Which product categories contributed most?
- Which regions or sellers were affected?
- What evidence supports the finding?
- What should the business investigate next?

### Core Business Question

> **Revenue changed. Why?**

AI Revenue Detective is designed to make this investigation more structured and reduce repetitive manual analysis.
